# Registration guidance

## [policy.registration.stars] Official registration channel

Students register through STARS according to the schedule announced by the Office of Academic Services.

## [policy.registration.add_drop] Add/Drop boundary

Courses may be added or dropped only during the designated Add/Drop period. The public undergraduate activity schedule identifies Teaching Weeks 1 and 2 as the general full-time Add/Drop window.

## [policy.registration.academic_load] Academic load

The AY2026–27 handbook describes an expected full-time load of 16–18 AU, subject to programme variation. It allows up to 3 AU above normal load subject to School Chair approval, and limits students on Academic Warning, Academic Probation, or with nil CGPA to normal load.

This cohort-specific wording must not be silently applied to earlier cohorts or merged with older registration guides that use different overload wording.

## [policy.registration.incoming_allocation] Incoming CCDS students

The CCDS public registration page says most incoming undergraduates have Core and ICC courses registered for them and should review their courses, timetable, BDE instructions, and NTU email.

## [unknown.registration.personalised] Personalised dates and detailed CCDS steps

UNKNOWN: personalised registration times and the detailed authenticated CCDS registration guide are not contained in the collected public sources.
