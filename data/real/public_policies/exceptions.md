# Exception, exemption, exchange and graduation guidance

## [policy.exception.transfer_exemption.baseline] Cohort-handbook transfer and exemption baseline

With prior approval of the **Chair concerned**, the selected cohort handbooks allow some courses taken at another university to transfer AU toward NTU degree requirements. External grades are not included in the NTU degree CGPA, only passed courses count toward AU, and transferred or exempted credit remains subject to the student's candidature and graduation requirements.

For courses completed before NTU admission, a student may apply to the School for exemption within the first semester of enrolment. The handbooks state that only credits and qualifications earned within five years of the application can be considered, credits already used for a degree at the same academic level cannot be transferred again, and there is no partial credit transfer. This baseline does not replace the more specific CCDS exemption and exchange procedures below.

## [policy.exception.course_exemption.olympiad] Olympiad-medallist exemption route

The CCDS exemption page identifies course exemptions for gold, silver or bronze medallists in specified international Olympiads and for specified awards in national informatics Olympiads. The table maps the competition to eligible courses, including combinations of SC1001, SC1004, SC1007, SC1008, SC2001 and the TAISP equivalents where stated.

Where the table marks the advanced algorithm exemption, the student must have completed its prerequisite courses and have CGPA 3.5 at application. The published application evidence is the relevant certificate, sent to `CCDS-Undergrad@ntu.edu.sg`. Applicability still depends on the exact competition, award level, programme and TAISP status in the source table.

## [policy.exception.course_exemption.preuniversity] A-Level, IB and NUS High exemption route

Students with qualifying Singapore GCE A-Level, International Baccalaureate or NUS High School Diploma results may be eligible for up to two selected first-year course exemptions. The public thresholds are grade A in the relevant H2 subject, grade 7 in the relevant IB Higher Level subject, or Major CAP 4.7 in the relevant NUS High subject.

The current table maps computing preparation to SC1001 for the listed CCDS single and double-degree programmes, and Physics to SC1013 for CE, Business and Computer Engineering, and Engineering and Economics. Applications beyond the first year are not accepted for SC10xx exemptions. Once a course has been read or exempted, the exemption cannot be granted, revoked or reversed.

## [policy.exception.course_exemption.poly.ay2026] AY2026 local-polytechnic BDE exemption

Starting with the AY2026 cohort, the CCDS page states that local-polytechnic diploma holders, regardless of diploma, receive a standardised 6 AU BDE exemption across the four named CCDS programmes: CSC, CE, DSAI and AISC. This wording must not be extended automatically to a double degree, joint programme, second-major overlay or earlier cohort not named by the rule.

## [policy.exception.course_exemption.poly.pre_ay2026] AY2025 and earlier local-polytechnic review

For AY2025 and earlier cohorts, the CCDS page describes exemption as a case-by-case review depending on the student's diploma, completed polytechnic courses, and CCDS conditions. It directs students to the accepted-diploma list in the curriculum structure. The rule does not promise an exemption merely because the student holds a local-polytechnic diploma.

## [policy.exception.course_exemption.freshmen_tests] Freshmen Exemption Tests

Year 1 CCDS students may be invited to optional exemption tests. The public page lists SC1001 and SC1004 in Semester 1 and SC1008 and SC2000 in Semester 2, with SC1004 and SC1008 not applicable to DSAI/AISC and SC2000 not applicable to DSAI. The College emails freshmen about registration; the tentative test timing is one to two weeks before semester and physical presence at NTU is required. A non-refundable fee is charged per test regardless of outcome.

A successful exemption counts toward graduation AU but carries no grade and does not count toward CGPA or the minimum letter-graded AU requirement. The exemption is permanent. Students already eligible through the listed pre-university results or Olympiad awards need not sit the tests.

## [policy.exception.exchange.withdrawal] Registration assistance after exchange withdrawal

After submitting an exchange-withdrawal application to OGEM, the student is to email `ccds-undergrad@ntu.edu.sg` with the requested course list and indexes. Registration is subject to availability and must be completed before the end of Add/Drop. This route does not authorise registration after Add/Drop and does not guarantee a place.

## [policy.exception.exchange.calendar_overlap] Exchange timing and return boundary

Students must compare the host and NTU calendars. The guide requires return no later than two weeks after the new NTU semester begins; a later return means the student cannot take NTU courses that semester and must instead consider semester LOA or, if possible, Professional Internship. Students remain responsible for registering the following NTU semester during their allocated registration and Add/Drop periods while overseas.

For PI immediately after exchange, the student gives CAO the exchange start and end dates for review and approval and must be able to complete PI before the next semester begins. Final-semester exchange is discouraged because transcript and credit-transfer timing may delay graduation and Convocation eligibility.

## [policy.exception.exchange.remote_exam] Narrow exchange remote-examination consideration

Students ordinarily attend classes and complete examinations at the host university. CCDS will consider a remote-examination arrangement only where the student was approved to take the exchange course, cannot sit the host examination because the academic calendars overlap, and the examination falls on a weekday excluding a public holiday between 8:30 am and 10:00 pm Singapore time. CCDS may still reject the request for logistical reasons; this is consideration, not an entitlement.

## [policy.exception.exchange.concurrent_projects] FYP, URECA and GEM VEnturer while on exchange

The second semester of FYP cannot be undertaken concurrently with overseas semester exchange because the oral presentation requires physical presence at NTU. A final-year student may undertake the first FYP semester concurrently only with the FYP supervisor's agreement and must submit the published MS Form with that approval as a PDF. Full or partial SUSEP students in Singapore have a parallel supervisor-agreement route. URECA concurrency likewise requires the URECA supervisor's agreement and the MS Form with the approval PDF.

For GEM VEnturer plus internship, the student sends the organisation supervisor's approval email to the CCDS Undergraduate Office for review and consideration by the CCDS Associate Dean (UG Education); the outcome is sent to NTU email. These narrow approvers must not be reused for unrelated exceptions.

## [policy.exception.exchange.course_matching] Course-matching submission and evidence

All matching requests must go through the course-matching portal for the relevant coordinator; the public CCDS contact page says requests by email or other unofficial channels will not be considered. Required evidence includes a detailed week-by-week syllabus, lecture/tutorial/lab/workshop contact hours, total teaching weeks and assessment modes. Insufficient information leads to rejection. A foreign course needs at least 39 formal contact hours to be eligible as a 3-AU course.

Students must obtain matching approval and satisfy CCDS prerequisites before exchange or concurrently with the higher-level exchange course. If a CCDS request remains unprocessed after two weeks, the guide directs the student to the coordinator copied on the notification or listed on the public exchange contacts page. Coordinators assess content similarity; students remain responsible for the correct NTU code, course type, AU and curriculum fit.

## [policy.exception.exchange.mapping_constraints] Exchange matching constraints

One foreign course cannot map to more than one NTU course, and approval can be withdrawn if submitted information is incorrect or untrue. Non-English-taught foreign courses can map only as BDE, not Core or MPE. Where no NTU code is an appropriate match, the guide permits generic BDE/UE or generic MPE subject to its limits; CCDS permits two generic MPEs, while only one can contribute to one CSC/BCG/CSEC specialisation. A separate PMC-approved MS Form appeal is required for generic MPE specialisation credit, and generic-MPE approval does not guarantee specialisation credit.

The guide requires all ICC courses, CE3004/CZ3004/SC2079 and SC3099 to be taken at NTU, and EG0001/EG1001 and HW0288 at NTU or another local university. It states that U22-and-later non-SPMS students cannot clear MHxxxx Core courses on exchange starting AY2024/25 Semester 1. Course availability at the host remains the student's responsibility.

## [policy.exception.exchange.credit_transfer_limits] Exchange credit-transfer maximums

For one semester exchange, the guide gives maximum transferable loads of 25 AU for double-degree programmes, 23 AU for Accelerated Bachelor Programme students, 18 AU for single-degree and non-ABP students, 24 AU for CN Yang Scholars and 20 AU for USP Scholars. Partial SUSEP NTU and NUS AU count together toward the applicable maximum, and BU5643 counts within it. The guide explicitly disallows overloading and says Pink Form overload requests will not be considered.

Special Term exchange is capped at 8 AU. Starting with AY2022–23 Special Term, Core courses are not eligible for transfer from overseas summer study. Winter Studies can clear only up to 3 AU of UE/BDE, cannot match a specific NTU course, and its transfer is managed by the GEM Discoverer Office rather than CCDS.

## [policy.exception.exchange.credit_transfer] Credit-transfer submission, effect and rejection

Credit transfer follows AU Projection and must be submitted before graduation; it cannot be added after the student graduates. Starting from AY2023 Semester 1 outbound programmes, only pre-approved passed courses are transferred and appear as `P` in Degree Audit and the final transcript. They count toward graduation AU but not CGPA or minimum letter-graded AU. FGO does not apply to host-university courses.

Published rejection reasons include mismatch between AU Projection, submission and transcript; unofficial, illegible or incomplete transcript; missing course-matching approval; no attempt or grade; unmet prerequisite; exceeding the AU maximum; an already-satisfied requirement category; duplicate completed course; and incorrect course type or code for matriculation year. The guide distinguishes SCxxxx for AY2021-and-later matriculation from CE/CZxxxx for AY2020-and-earlier. A student may be required to correct and resubmit.

## [policy.exception.exchange.pending_transfer] Pending exchange-credit resolution

CCDS starts processing after it receives an official transcript for verification and states that reflection in Degree Audit takes approximately four weeks. Once finalised and reflected, amendments are not permitted.

If registration or Add/Drop occurs before the official transcript is released or before the exchange course appears in Degree Audit, the student emails `ccds-undergrad@ntu.edu.sg` with the unofficial transcript and three mappings: the NTU course requested, the NTU prerequisite completed during exchange, and the corresponding foreign course. CCDS verifies and issues the prerequisite waiver. This is the only collected public CCDS prerequisite-waiver route.

Students who become FYP-eligible only after transfer may ask `ccds-fyp@ntu.edu.sg` for FYP Selection Portal access. A student with pending transfer from final-semester exchange is to notify the CCDS Undergraduate Office so the multi-office transfer can be expedited for Convocation eligibility.

## [policy.exception.icc.registration] ICC registration and study-plan deviation

The public ICC FAQ says the student's School, not ICC Office, registers ICC courses and decides their planned year and semester. For the six-course structure described on that page, all six are compulsory and cannot be dropped; a student seeking to deviate from the plan must approach the School. This current FAQ must not overwrite a different ICC structure in an earlier cohort curriculum.

## [policy.exception.icc.cc0006_clash] Narrow CC0006 quiz and BDE clash route

For a BDE that clashes with the CC0006 week-12 compulsory in-person quiz slot, the student first obtains permission from the other course's coordinator to miss that course's week-12 lesson. The student then emails ICC Office, states awareness that the quiz remains compulsory and that the other class will be missed, attaches the coordinator approval, and copies the School undergraduate academic office. If complete, ICC Office tells the School it has no objection on condition the student attends the week-12 CC0006 quiz in person.

This does not waive the CC0006 quiz and is not a general timetable-clash policy. Week 13 is reserved only for a make-up quiz where week-12 absence has a valid approved LOA.

## [policy.exception.icc.short_loa] Narrow public ICC absence and short-LOA evidence

For ICC class or quiz absence, the FAQ tells the student to notify the instructor with documentary proof such as medical certificate, hospitalisation leave or authorised NTU representation, and to submit proof to the School for short-LOA approval. It says the absence is invalid without an approved short LOA and describes OAS approval. The complete LOA policy and procedure are linked on Student Intranet and are not public in this package.

## [policy.exception.restricted_repeat] Restricted-repeat eligibility boundary

The selected cohort handbooks say a final-year student who failed a Core course not offered in the following semester may be permitted a restricted repeat only when no replacement course is available in that following semester and the student is deemed to have sufficient AU to meet graduation requirements. The word “may” is preserved: eligibility is not approval, and the public handbooks do not give the CCDS form or delegated decision path.

## [policy.graduation.public_status] Public graduation status and conferment

The public Graduation Matters page says final-semester results show graduand status. Undergraduate degrees are conferred only after graduation requirements are met, on published conferment dates after Semester 1, Semester 2 or Special Term; exchange-credit or internship completion can use additional conferment dates. Conferment may take up to two months. A certification letter becomes available through One Stop only after the relevant status/conferment conditions, and degree certificates and transcripts are not issued before formal conferment.

This downstream process is not an exception approval. A final-year student must also check the annually updated Convocation deadline and clear outstanding fees; the public key-activities schedule warns that late exchange, attachment or final results can defer Convocation to the following year.
