# Exception guidance

## [policy.exception.exchange_prerequisite_waiver] Pending exchange-credit prerequisite waiver

CCDS publishes one narrow prerequisite-waiver route. When registration or Add/Drop occurs before an exchange result is available or reflected in Degree Audit, the student emails the CCDS Undergraduate Office for verification and waiver issuance. The published evidence is an unofficial transcript plus the NTU course requested, the NTU prerequisite completed during exchange, and the corresponding foreign course.

This route is limited to the documented pending exchange-credit context.

## [policy.exception.restricted_repeat] Restricted-repeat eligibility boundary

The AY2026–27 handbook says a final-year student who failed a core course not offered in the following semester may be eligible for restricted repeat when no replacement exists and the student has sufficient AUs to meet graduation requirements.

## [unknown.exception.general_prerequisite_waiver] General prerequisite waiver

UNKNOWN: the collected public CCDS sources do not define a general prerequisite-waiver procedure outside the exchange-credit case.

## [unknown.exception.after_add_drop] Registration after Add/Drop closes

UNKNOWN: the collected public CCDS sources do not define a general late-registration exception process after Add/Drop closes, its supporting documents, decision maker, service time, or guaranteed outcome.

## [unknown.exception.restricted_repeat_process] Restricted-repeat submission workflow

UNKNOWN: the public handbook provides eligibility conditions but not the exact submission channel, supporting documents, or delegated approver for CCDS.
