# Approval and routing structure

## [policy.approval.overload_chair] School Chair for AY2026–27 overload

The AY2026–27 handbook identifies School Chair approval for up to 3 AU above the normal semester load. This does not establish the request form, supporting evidence, delegated reviewer, or rules for another cohort.

## [policy.role.ccds_registration_team] CCDS Course Registration Team

The public CCDS registration page routes incoming-student registration issues to the CCDS Course Registration Team. This is a support and routing role, not evidence that the team is the final approver for every exception.

## [policy.role.ccds_undergraduate_office] CCDS Undergraduate Office

Public CCDS pages route curriculum, academic-policy, programme-specific, and full-time course-registration enquiries to the CCDS Undergraduate Office. A contact role must not be treated as proof of final approval authority.

## [policy.role.oas] Office of Academic Services

The CCDS student-enquiries page routes university registration policies and rules to the Office of Academic Services.

## [policy.role.academic_mentor] Academic Mentor

The CCDS student-enquiries page routes academic advice to the student's Academic Mentor. It does not identify the mentor as an exception approver.

## [unknown.approval.general_exception_chain] General CCDS approval chain

UNKNOWN: the collected public sources do not establish whether a course coordinator, programme committee, undergraduate office, or another role must approve a general late-registration or prerequisite-waiver case.
