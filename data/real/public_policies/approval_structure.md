# Approval and routing structure

## [policy.approval.chair_concerned] Chair concerned

The selected cohort handbooks use the exact role **Chair concerned** for two boundaries relevant to the problem: approval to take BDE from another School or to transfer courses from another university, and approval for up to 3 AU above the normal semester load. The handbook does not name the individual, form, delegated reviewer or routing sequence. This corpus therefore does not relabel the role “School Chair” or assume that one Chair handles every CCDS exception.

## [policy.role.ccds_registration_team] CCDS Course Registration Team

The public CCDS starting-university page routes incoming-student course-registration or timetable anomalies to `CCDS-REG@ntu.edu.sg`. It is a support and routing contact for the incoming context, not evidence that the team approves overloads, prerequisite waivers, late registration or graduation substitutions.

## [policy.role.ccds_undergraduate_office] CCDS Undergraduate Office

Public CCDS pages route full-time course registration, curriculum, academic-policy, programme, short-leave and selected withdrawal enquiries to `CCDS-undergrad@ntu.edu.sg`. The exchange guide uses the same office for exchange-withdrawal registration help, pending-transfer prerequisite-waiver verification, and final-semester transfer notification. A contact or verification role is not proof of final approval authority outside each documented route.

## [policy.role.ccds_part_time] CCDS part-time undergraduate route

The public student-enquiries page routes part-time undergraduate course-registration matters to `ccds-pt@ntu.edu.sg`. Full-time routing must not be substituted automatically for a BTech Computing student, and this contact does not by itself establish an approval role.

## [policy.role.oas_registration] Office of Academic Services registration-policy route

The CCDS student-enquiries page routes policies and rules on registration to the Office of Academic Services at `regn_course@ntu.edu.sg`. This is distinct from a CCDS programme/curriculum enquiry and from an IT fault. The page does not identify OAS as the final approver for every student exception.

## [policy.role.it_service_desk] IT Service Desk

Network, STARS, PIN and similar system faults are routed to the IT Service Desk through the public CCDS contact page. A technical failure should not be misrouted as an academic-policy request; conversely, the Service Desk does not decide whether a student may waive a prerequisite or exceed load.

## [policy.role.one_stop] One Stop @ SAC

The public CCDS contact page routes tuition-fee, hostel-fee and other payment issues to One Stop @ SAC. Graduation Matters also routes certification-letter requests through One Stop once the relevant graduate or conferment status is available. These transactional services do not approve a CCDS academic exception.

## [policy.role.academic_mentor] Academic Mentor

The public CCDS contact page routes academic advice and student counselling to the Academic Mentor. The exchange guide also allows a mentor or a professor who knows the student to provide a support/recommendation letter where a host application requires one. Neither source identifies the mentor as an academic-exception approver.

## [policy.role.exchange_matching_coordinator] Exchange course-matching coordinators

Course matching must be submitted through the matching portal to the coordinator responsible for the subject area. The coordinators assess similarity between the foreign and NTU course; they do not decide the student's curriculum category, AU need or general registration exception. If a CCDS request remains unprocessed for two weeks, the student may contact the coordinator named in the notification or current public coordinator list.

## [policy.role.exchange.cao] Career and Attachment Office for PI timing after exchange

For Professional Internship immediately after an exchange, the guide directs the student to provide exchange dates to CAO for review and approval of whether PI can start and finish before the subsequent semester. This is a specific internship-timing role, not approval authority over unrelated registration cases.

## [policy.role.exchange.project_supervisors] FYP and URECA supervisors

The FYP supervisor's agreement is required for the narrow first-semester FYP concurrency route, and the URECA supervisor's agreement is required for concurrent URECA. Their email approval is attached to the published MS Form. Supervisor agreement is a required input to those routes and is not a general course-registration approval.

## [policy.approval.exchange.associate_dean] Associate Dean for GEM VEnturer plus internship

For concurrent GEM VEnturer exchange and internship, the student sends the organisation supervisor's approval to the CCDS Undergraduate Office for review and consideration by the CCDS Associate Dean (UG Education). The outcome is communicated by NTU email. This role is not generalised beyond that named case.

## [policy.approval.exchange.pmc] Programme Management Committee for specialisation appeal

After a generic MPE matching request is approved, a CSC/BCG/CSEC student seeking to count it toward a specialisation submits a separate MS Form appeal for review and approval by the Programme Management Committee. Generic-MPE matching approval alone does not guarantee specialisation credit. PMC authority is not inferred for other substitutions or appeals.

## [policy.role.icc_course_coordinator] ICC instructor and course coordinator

The ICC FAQ routes a course-content query to the class instructor or the course coordinator. Its named coordinator roster is labelled AY2025 Semester 1 and therefore must not be used as a current personnel directory without refresh. The role becomes an approval input only where a specific public workflow says so.

## [policy.approval.icc.cc0006_clash] CC0006 clash roles

For the narrow CC0006 week-12 quiz/BDE clash, the other course's coordinator first gives permission to miss that course's week-12 lesson. The student then informs ICC Office, attaches the permission, and copies the School undergraduate academic office. ICC Office can state that it has no objection subject to in-person CC0006 quiz attendance. This is not a general clash waiver and does not remove the compulsory quiz.

## [policy.role.short_loa] School and OAS in the narrow public short-LOA route

The ICC FAQ directs absence evidence to the School and states that an approved short LOA from OAS is required for the absence to be valid. The CCDS contact page routes short-leave enquiries to the Undergraduate Office. The public corpus does not expose the full policy, form, delegated School reviewer or all OAS decision rules.

## [policy.role.graduation] University conferment and graduation services

The University formally confers degrees after requirements are met. Graduation Matters routes post-status certification letters through One Stop and explains that degree certificates and transcripts follow conferment. These downstream roles cannot repair a missing curriculum requirement or approve course registration; the academic issue must first use its applicable CCDS/OAS route.

## [unknown.approval.general_exception_chain] General CCDS exception chain

UNKNOWN: the collected public sources do not establish one universal sequence involving course coordinator, Undergraduate Office, programme committee, Chair concerned and OAS for late registration, general prerequisite waiver, overload, substitution or restricted repeat. Only the route explicitly documented for the matching case may be used.

## [unknown.approval.delegation_sla] Delegation, escalation and service times

UNKNOWN: public sources do not establish which named individual currently holds a general delegated authority, when a case escalates, required internal endorsements, response-time commitments, appeal levels or a guaranteed decision date.
